About This Tool

This interactive explorer accompanies the published manuscript:

Anand S et al. Subtypes of AL Amyloidosis and Cumulative Incidence of Mortality and ESKD. American Journal of Hematology, 2024. doi:10.1002/ajh.27472

Methods Summary

  • 2,067 AL amyloidosis patients from the Boston University Center for Amyloidosis
  • Unsupervised clustering via Consensus Clustering Plus on 33 quantitative laboratory variables
  • Three subtypes identified: Low-risk, Intermediate, High-risk
  • Subtype prediction model: XGBoost trained on 8 clinical measurements (5-fold CV acc ≈ 82%)
  • Survival: Kaplan-Meier with log-rank testing; Cox proportional hazards models

Staging Systems Shown

  • Mayo 2004 cardiac staging: based on troponin T and BNP thresholds
  • BU (BNP-based) cardiac staging: Boston University BNP-based cardiac staging
  • Palladini renal staging: based on eGFR and proteinuria

Data & Code

All analysis code is available at github.com/shankara-a/amyloidosis_bmc_2023 . Patient-level data is not published to protect privacy. The prediction model runs entirely in your browser — no data is transmitted.
